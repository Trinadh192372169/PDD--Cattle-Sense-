About CattleSense

CattleSense is an intelligent livestock management solution for farmers and cattle owners. It brings together image-based breed recognition, record tracking, and a user-friendly mobile experience in a single app ecosystem.

Mission

To make livestock management simpler, faster, and more data-driven for modern farmers.

Vision

To support healthier herds, easier breed identification, and better farm planning using AI.
